## 🛑 Part 1: The Emergency Fridge Cheatsheet
*Print this page and tape it to your refrigerator.*

### 1. Know Your Dog's Baseline Vitals
Write these down when your dog is healthy and resting. 
- **Resting Heart Rate:** ____ BPM (Normal: 60-140 based on size)
- **Breathing Rate:** ____ Breaths/Min (Normal: 10-30. Over 40 while resting is an emergency)
- **Gum Color:** _________ (Should be bubblegum pink. White, blue, or dark red is an emergency)
- **Weight:** ____ lbs/kg (Date weighed: _________)

### 2. The 3-Second Capillary Refill Test
If you think your dog is sick, press your thumb firmly into their pink gums until the spot turns white, then release. 
- **Normal:** The pink color should return in **under 2 seconds**. 
- **Emergency:** If it takes 3+ seconds to turn pink again, they are experiencing poor circulation or shock. Go to the ER.

### 3. Toxic Foods & Items to Avoid
- Xylitol (Birch Sugar) - Found in sugar-free gum & peanut butter. Highly fatal.
- Grapes & Raisins - Causes rapid kidney failure.
- Macadamia Nuts & Onions/Garlic.
- Ibuprofen/Advil - NEVER give human painkillers to dogs.

## 📋 Part 2: The Vet-Ready Record Checklist
*Use this checklist to organize your records before your next vet visit.*

When your dog gets sick, you only get 15 minutes with the vet. Do not waste that time trying to remember what month they started limping. 

- [ ] **Create a Timeline:** Write down the exact date you first noticed the symptom. "He started limping on Tuesday morning" is much better than "He's been limping for a while."
- [ ] **Film the Symptom:** If they are coughing, limping, or shaking, take a 10-second video. Dogs often hide their symptoms in the exam room because they are scared. The video is proof.
- [ ] **List All Current Medications/Supplements:** Write down the exact brand names, milligrams (mg), and time of day you give them. (e.g., "Dasuquin with MSM, 1 chew at 8 AM").
- [ ] **Diet Details:** Know the exact brand and protein source of their food (e.g., "Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin & Stomach - Salmon").
- [ ] **Use the PetHealthNotes App:** Plug all of these symptoms and medications into the free PetHealthNotes tracker app to generate a clean, 1-page PDF report to hand to the vet receptionist.
