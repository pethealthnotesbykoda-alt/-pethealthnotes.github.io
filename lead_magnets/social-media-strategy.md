# Month 2: Short-Form Social Strategy

**Goal:** Drive 500+ top-of-funnel users per week to the Free Lead Magnets (Checklists) and Webinar Registration using high-virality, low-production TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

## The Format: "The Vet Secret" & "Mistakes I Made"
Do not do dancing videos. Do not do generic "cute dog" videos.
Your entire E-E-A-T is built on **Authority and Empathy**. 
You will use a "Talking Head" format (you holding your phone, talking directly to the camera, maybe B-roll of your dog).

### Video 1: The "Silent Pain" Hook (Mobility)
**Visual:** You sitting on the floor with your dog, looking directly at the camera.
**Hook (0-3s):** "If your dog is over 7 years old, they are probably hiding their joint pain from you right now."
