Active Critical Recalls

1. Answers Pet Food (Salmonella/Listeria)

Issue: Potential contamination with Salmonella and Listeria monocytogenes.

Affected: Raw frozen pet food products.

2. Raw Bistro Pet Fare (Salmonella)

Issue: Salmonella contamination risk.

Affected: Frozen grass-fed beef dog food (Best By Aug 27, 2026).

3. Pedigree / Mars Petcare

Issue: Foreign material contamination.

Affected: Specific canned dog food lots.

What Should You Do?

  • Stop feeding the product immediately.
  • Check the lot number on your package against the FDA list.
  • Contact the manufacturer for a refund or replacement.
  • Watch your pet for vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy, or loss of appetite.
