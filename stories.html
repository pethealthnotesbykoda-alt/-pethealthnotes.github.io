DON's Senior Journey

Arthritis & Mobility

Watching DON slip on our hardwood floors broke my heart. I thought he was just 'getting old'. Once we laid down yoga mats and started giving him joint supplements, it was like someone turned the clock back two years. Don't underestimate how much environmental changes help.

Max's IVDD Recovery

Spinal Health

When Max suddenly couldn't use his back legs, I panicked. The ER vet diagnosed him with Stage 3 IVDD. We opted for strict crate rest and laser therapy. The first 4 weeks were a nightmare of carrying him out to potty, but by week 6, he was walking again. For anyone going through this: patience is everything. Stick to the crate rest strictly.

— Sarah M., Chicago

Bella's Parvo Scare

Puppy Health

I skipped one booster shot because she 'seemed fine' and we took her to the pet store. Three days later, she stopped eating and the diarrhea started. She spent 5 days in the ICU. It cost us $4,000 to save her life. Please, please finish the vaccine schedule before taking your puppy out.

— David T., Seattle

Cooper's Separation Anxiety

Behavioral

Cooper used to destroy the blinds every time I left the house. We tried everything. What finally worked was a combination of a Snuggle Puppy (with the heartbeat), leaving the TV on a nature channel, and practicing leaving for just 5 minutes at a time. It took 6 months of training, but now he just sleeps on the couch.

— Emma L., Austin
"
🐕

Luna's Food Allergies

Diet & Allergies

Luna had constant ear infections and was licking her paws raw. Our vet suggested an elimination diet. It turns out she was severely allergic to chicken—which is in almost everything! Switching her to a limited-ingredient salmon food cleared up her skin completely within a month.

— Mark & Jess, Boston
