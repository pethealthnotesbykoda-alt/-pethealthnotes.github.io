BLOCK 5 — ROOT CAUSE SECTION

Why French Bulldogs Are Especially Vulnerable

French Bulldogs are prone to skin allergies due to genetics. Brachycephalic dogs are more susceptible to heat stress. Folded skin areas can trap moisture, leading to infections.
