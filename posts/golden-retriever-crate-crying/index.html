How to Stop a Golden Retriever Puppy From Crying Crated Downstairs

BLOCK 1: An Emotional Intro

As a 10-year pet parent, I’ve been in your shoes - waking up to the heartbreaking sound of my Golden Retriever puppy crying downstairs in their crate. It’s a feeling of helplessness and worry that’s hard to shake off. But with the right approach and tools, you can help your puppy feel safe and secure, and get a good night’s sleep yourself.

BLOCK 2: Emergency Callout Box

If your Golden Retriever puppy is hyperventilating, injuring their paws or teeth trying to chew through the metal bars, or crying continuously for over an hour, stop immediately. These are signs of severe clinical separation anxiety, and you should consult your veterinarian or a certified veterinary behaviorist.
