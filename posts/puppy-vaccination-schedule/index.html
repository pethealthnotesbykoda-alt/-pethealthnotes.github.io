Puppy Vaccination Schedule: Complete Timeline + FAQs

Introduction to Puppy Vaccinations

As a 10-year pet parent, I remember the confusion and concern I had when my first puppy received its vaccinations. The numerous abbreviations like DA2PP, Bordetella, and Leptospira seemed like a foreign language. The veterinarian explained each vaccine, but I left feeling overwhelmed and unsure if I had grasped everything. This article aims to provide a clear, comprehensive explanation of the puppy vaccination schedule, written for real pet owners, not medical students.

Urgent: Don’t Miss These Vaccinations

If you’ve recently brought home a puppy, it’s crucial to understand the vaccination schedule to ensure your puppy’s health and safety. Missing a vaccine or not following the recommended timeline can leave your puppy vulnerable to life-threatening diseases.

Core vs. Non-Core Vaccines

Core vaccines are essential for every dog, regardless of lifestyle, as they protect against widespread, severe, or zoonotic diseases. Non-core vaccines are recommended based on lifestyle, region, or risk.

The Puppy Vaccination Timeline

The puppy vaccination series typically starts at 6-8 weeks old and continues until 14-16 weeks old. The timeline includes:

  • 6-8 weeks: First DA2PP
  • 10-12 weeks: Second DA2PP + optional non-core vaccines
  • 14-16 weeks: Third DA2PP + first rabies
  • 12-16 months: First annual booster
  • Every 1-3 years: Adult boosters
