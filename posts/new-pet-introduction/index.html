Introducing a New Pet to Your Home: A 7-Day Transition Plan

Introduction to Introducing a New Pet

As a pet parent of over 10 years, I’ve learned that introducing a new pet to your household can be a daunting task. You want to ensure a smooth and stress-free transition for both your new and existing pets. In this article, we’ll walk you through a step-by-step guide on how to introduce your new pet to your household, covering everything from preparation to long-term cohabitation.

Emergency Callout

If you notice any signs of distress, aggression, or fear-based behaviors during introductions, consult your veterinarian immediately. These signs include persistent aggression, injuries, changes in appetite, vomiting, diarrhea, or signs of stress such as panting, pacing, or hiding.

The 7-Day Introduction Plan

The key to a successful introduction is a gradual and controlled approach. Here’s a step-by-step guide to follow:

  • Day 1: Arrive, Base Camp Only - Bring your new pet directly to their base camp room. Allow them to explore and settle at their own pace.
  • Days 2-3: Scent Exchange (For Multi-Pet Households) - Swap bedding between your new pet and existing pets to facilitate scent exchange.
  • Days 3-4: Expand Territory (Supervised) - Allow your new pet to explore one or two additional rooms while your existing pets are in a separate space.
  • Days 4-5: First Sight Introduction (Through Barrier) - Introduce your pets through a barrier, such as a baby gate or glass door, to allow them to become familiar with each other’s presence.
  • Days 5-6: First Off-Leash Meeting (Dogs) / Free Sniff (Cats) - Allow your pets to interact with each other in a controlled environment, supervising their behavior and body language.
  • Day 7+: Supervised Co-habitation - Gradually increase the amount of time your pets spend together, always supervising their interactions.
