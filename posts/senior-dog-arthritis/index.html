Understanding Arthritis in Senior Dogs

Arthritis, also known as osteoarthritis or degenerative joint disease, affects an estimated 80% of dogs over the age of eight. Dogs are stoic creatures and often hide their pain, making it challenging for owners to recognize the early signs of arthritis.

Early Warning Signs of Arthritis in Dogs

By knowing what to look for, you can identify the early signs of arthritis in your dog. These include:

  • Reluctance to jump
  • Morning stiffness that warms up
  • Sleeping more, playing less
  • Losing interest in activities they used to love
  • Irritability or snapping when touched
  • Licking or chewing a specific joint
  • Changes in gait
  • Muscle wasting
