Indoor Cat Enrichment: 10 Vet-Approved Activities Under $20

BLOCK 1: An Emotional Intro

As a fellow pet parent of over a decade, I’ve seen firsthand the devastating effects of boredom on our feline friends. The constant meowing, the destructive behavior, the lack of interest in play – it’s heartbreaking to watch. But what if I told you that boredom is more than just a minor issue? It’s a serious health risk that can lead to chronic understimulation, stress, and even life-threatening conditions like feline idiopathic cystitis.

BLOCK 2: Emergency Callout Box

If your cat exhibits sudden behavioral changes, such as not eating, not using the litter box, or excessive vocalization, seek veterinary attention immediately. Many medical conditions present as behavioral changes in cats, and early detection is crucial for effective treatment.

BLOCK 6: Signs Your Cat Needs More Enrichment

Here are some signs that your cat needs more enrichment:

  • Over-grooming or bald patches appearing
  • Aggression or redirected biting that’s new
  • Waking you up at 3am with zoomies or crying
  • Increased vocalization without obvious cause
  • Not using the litter box (always check for medical causes first)
  • Excessive weight gain despite normal portions
  • Hiding more than usual or loss of interest in play

BLOCK 7: Conclusion and Final Call-to-Action
