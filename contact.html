Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get advice about my specific pet's health problem?

We're not able to provide individual veterinary advice — that requires a proper examination by a licensed vet who knows your pet. Our articles are educational guides for general information. For anything specific to your pet, please consult your veterinarian.

How do I suggest a topic for a new article?

Use the contact form above and select "Suggest a Topic." Tell us what you searched for and couldn't find a good answer to — that's exactly the kind of gap we want to fill. We read all suggestions and prioritise the most common requests.

Are your articles reviewed by a veterinarian?

Yes. All health-related articles on Pet Health Notes are reviewed by a licensed veterinarian for medical accuracy before publication. The site itself is run by a passionate pet parent — not a vet — but we take accuracy seriously enough to have professional review built into our process.

Is the Pet Health Notes app free?

The app has a free tier that covers core features including pet profiles, journal entries, and basic health tracking. Premium features are available via subscription. Learn more about the app →

Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content?

We currently do not accept unsolicited guest posts or paid placements that aren't clearly disclosed. If you're interested in a legitimate editorial collaboration, email us at support@pethealthnotes.com with details and we'll consider it on a case-by-case basis.
